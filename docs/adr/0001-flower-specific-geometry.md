# Share geometry primitives and specialize flower forms

Flora will share geometric primitives where they provide useful, predictable control, and use flower-specific formulas where a shared model cannot express the characteristic shape. A single deformation model currently couples several aspects of petal shape and flower profile; tuning that model alone has not produced satisfactory profiles across the specimens.

Petal curvature and radial layout will be independently adjustable, with visual curve editors for petal side profile and height across the flower radius. The alternative of continuing to force every specimen through one formula was rejected because recognizable flower structures differ and their controls must remain understandable. Artistic variation is allowed within those recognizable structures.
