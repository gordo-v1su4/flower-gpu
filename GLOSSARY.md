# Flora

Flora is an interactive flower study. Its specimens are judged against the recognizable forms of real flowers.

## Language

**Specimen**:
A particular flower represented in Flora, such as Chrysanthemum or Copper Rose.

**Profile**:
The side view of a specimen, showing its height, silhouette, petal curvature, and the underside of the flower head.

**Top view**:
The view looking down into the flower head, showing the arrangement of petals and the center.

**Reference views**:
The front, side, and top views of a specimen, plus a low upward angle that reveals the underside. These views are compared visually with the target flower.

**Bloom cycle**:
The change between a closed bud and an open flower, including closing back into the bud.

**Material audition**:
A visual comparison of candidate surface appearances on a specimen, used to choose its texture and material qualities.

**Petal curve**:
The shape of a petal from its attachment at the base through its body to its tip.

**Radial layout**:
The arrangement of petals from the center of the flower head to its outer edge, including how their height, size, and orientation vary across that distance.
